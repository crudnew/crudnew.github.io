WHO AM I?
I'm a UX/UI designer and researcher with a foundation in computer science and business studies, focused on building accessible and user-centered digital experiences.

BACKGROUND:
I'm a first-year student completing a Master of Information, concentrating in User Experience Design, at the University of Toronto, Canada.

I earned a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science with a minor in Business Studies, in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada.

This combination gives me a strong foundation alongside an understanding of product strategy and user needs, while allowing me to work fluently alongside designers, researchers, and engineers.

Why Me?

What I Bring

  • A strong academic foundation, allowing me to design with both users and feasibility in mind.
  • A research-informed design approach, using interviews, testing, and synthesis to guide decision making.
  • The ability to collaborate and communicate effectively and enthusiastically across multiple disciplines, helping align researchers, designers, and engineers while keeping spirits high.

How I Work

I value collaboration, curiosity, creativity, and grounding design decisions in evidence. I enjoy working in diverse environments and see every project as an opportunity for personal and professional growth.

Open communication, respect, and humor (where appropriate) help teams thrive, and I aspire to be someone who can create a positive and productive team environment.

Inclusivity

I believe accessibility is extremely important, but also fun to implement. Dark mode, high contrast, and reduced motion are wired into every page on this site (with more to come), with WCAG checks throughout my process. If a feature can be made more usable for someone, that's the version I want to ship.
